Rhianna is a musician. She had her first #1 hit in 2006.

Rhianna is an entrepreneur and started her own beauty brand called "Fenty" (she actually started it with LVMH) in 2017. The estimated turnover per year is close to 600 million USD.

A$AP Rocky is a rapper.

A$AP Rocky is engaged to Rhianna, and they are (probably) married. They have two kids together.

ASAP Rocky launched a song called "Riot" on July 20th, 2023.

In "Riot," he raps about a lip balm 2:37 into the song (Lip balm? In a video like this?):

> **"FLOSSY GLOSSY. NAH BITCH THIS AINT LIP GLOSS. LUX BALM UP IN MY PALM. NEW COLLAB WITH MY BABY MOM"**
