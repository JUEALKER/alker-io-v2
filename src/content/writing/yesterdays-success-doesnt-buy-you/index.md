Waiting for things to get better.
Waiting for the world to go back to “normal.”
Waiting for the good old times to return.
But deep inside, you already know:

They won’t.

The world has changed—and it keeps changing fast.
You don’t know what tomorrow will look like.
Nobody does.

But one thing is clear: If you just sit still and hope, you’re already behind.

It doesn’t matter if you’re an employee or an entrepreneur.

You have to challenge your current model.

Ask yourself:
 • How will I make money in 6, 12, or 24 months from now?
 • Does my business model still work?
 • Am I providing value—today and tomorrow?

Because that’s the only thing that matters.

Not what you built yesterday.
Not what you once earned.
Only the value you provide going forward.

If the answer is yes—you’re probably safe.

If the answer is no—then that’s the only question that should keep you up at night:

**How do I increase the value I provide?**

Everything else is just noise.
