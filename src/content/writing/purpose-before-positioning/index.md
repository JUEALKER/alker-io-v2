What dies is not you. It is the role. For fifteen years the answer to the question of who you are was printed on the back of a shirt. The number, the position, the club. When that is taken away, most people discover they never built an identity underneath it.

#### They were the role, completely, and now the role is gone.

The instinct at that point is to reach for a new shirt as fast as possible. Entrepreneur. Investor. Founder. A new label to replace the old one, so the question of who you are has an answer again. This is positioning without purpose. It produces a brand with no spine, a confident answer to the wrong question. You can spot these people easily. They have a title and a deck and no idea what they are for.

> Purpose is almost never a business category. It is a human thread that runs under the football and usually predates it. The place you came from. The community you belong to. A craft, a question, a kind of person you keep being drawn to. The thing you cared about before anyone paid you to play, that never fully went away even when football took up all the room.

You find it by subtraction, not addition. The wrong question is what you should add to football to build a second career. The right question is what is left when you remove football entirely. Take away the game and the money and the status, and see what you still care about when nothing depends on it. Whatever survives that subtraction is the thread. It is usually quieter and less impressive than the label you would have reached for, and it is the only thing worth building on.

This is why the work happens at twenty-three and not thirty-three. At twenty-three you can follow the thread slowly and cheaply, with no pressure for it to replace an income, and you can be wrong a few times without cost. At thirty-three you are choosing a second life in the emotional state of grief, under financial pressure, which is close to the worst possible condition for a clear decision. The players who transition well almost always started while it felt completely unnecessary.

The method for finding the thread is not a strategy offsite. It is curiosity about specific people, specific places, and specific problems.

#### Follow what you keep returning to when no one is watching and no one is paying.

The people who do this best never had a brand strategy. They were curious about particular people and places, and let the work follow the curiosity instead of chasing it. Football can be the entry point into that curiosity rather than the whole of it. The game gives you a reason to be in a room. The purpose is what you do once you are there.

Everything that comes later in this series depends on this part being right.

**Positioning gives your purpose an edge. The network gives it people. The platform gives it a voice. Partnerships and capital give it fuel.** All of it points somewhere, and this is where it points. Build the positioning before the purpose and you get a sharp answer to a question that was never yours. Get the purpose right and everything downstream finally has somewhere to go.
