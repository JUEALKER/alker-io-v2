The nine parts laid out the how. Purpose and the second education to know what
you are building and to learn fast enough to build it.

But the tools were never the point. Strip all of it back and one thing is left.

> Football gave you something most people never find. A thing you would have
> done for nothing.

<Statement>Protect the money, fill the calendar, stay busy. A managed decline dressed up as a second act.</Statement>

That is the belief this whole series exists to break. **The real work is to find
the next thing you can chase with the same hunger.**

<Statement>The whistle has gone. Ninety minutes was only ever the game.</Statement>
